What shoppers often want to know

What exactly is Jetterix?

Jetterix is a compact attachment designed to connect to a garden hose and direct the available water through a more focused spray for routine outdoor rinsing and cleaning.

Does it create its own water pressure?

It does not use a motor or pump. It works with the water pressure already available through your household hose connection and changes the spray pattern and concentration.

Is it the same as an electric pressure washer?

No. It is a hose attachment rather than a motorized pressure washer. Its main advantages are compact size, simple connection and portability.

What affects the results?

Household water pressure, hose diameter and condition, connector fit, spray setting, distance from the surface and the type of dirt can all affect performance.

Can it be used on different outdoor surfaces?

The seller presents Jetterix for multiple exterior applications. Always follow the current instructions, begin at a safe distance and test a small, inconspicuous area before broader use.

Does it require electricity, fuel or a separate machine?

No electricity or fuel is required. The product is designed to work as a garden-hose attachment.
